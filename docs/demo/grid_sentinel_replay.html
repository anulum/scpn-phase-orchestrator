Claim boundary (read before quoting)
  • The threshold was calibrated on this system’s own pre-onset ambient windows at a 10% false-alarm target — per-system calibration is the deployment step, not a fallback.
  • The calibration corpus is small (six ambient windows, three events, p = 0.33): this is a demonstration of the sealed live pipeline, not a statistical generalisation claim. The powered leg (WECC 240-bus) is in progress.
  • The sentinel is review-only: it observes and seals; it never actuates.
