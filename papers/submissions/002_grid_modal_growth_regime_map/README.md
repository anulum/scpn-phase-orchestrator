# A domain-specific modal-growth detector and the eigenvalue regime map

The positive / physical manuscript of the pair: a detector reading the
canonical wide-area-monitoring instability quantity (the exponential
growth rate of the most unstable bus's voltage-deviation envelope)
clears the matched-false-alarm bar on the PSML corpus where every
generic detector is at chance, and is then bounded by streaming
recalibration, two cross-dataset corpora, transfer boundaries, and
external validation against five independent eigenvalue ground truths,
yielding a regime map (envelope growth sizes oscillatory modes,
autocorrelation non-oscillatory ones).

[10.5281/zenodo.22113116](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22113116)
